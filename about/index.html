About

Andy Alexander — Linux systems engineer

I'm a Linux systems engineer with 15+ years of production experience, most of it keeping other people's infrastructure running: high-volume transactional email platforms, globally distributed web infrastructure for ESPN.com and ESPN3.com at Disney, ERP rollouts, and more Nagios instances than I can count. I got my start supporting Ford Motor Company's infrastructure through a Ciber/NTT contract back in 2002, and I've been somewhere in the Linux/networking/storage stack ever since.

Since 2022 I've been running that same discipline at home instead of at an employer — designing, breaking, fixing, and documenting a real multi-site environment because it's the best way I know to keep the skills sharp and prove they're current. This site and the runbooks linked from it are the result: three Proxmox hosts, ZFS storage on TrueNAS SCALE, OPNsense routing over XGS-PON fiber, a WireGuard overlay connecting multiple sites, redundant DNS, and backups that actually get verified. Nothing here is a lab exercise from a course — it's the infrastructure I depend on daily, documented the way I'd want it documented if someone else had to pick it up at 2am.
