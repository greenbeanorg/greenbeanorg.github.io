Andy Alexander — Homelab & Infrastructure

Multi-site homelab infrastructure, documented as production-style runbooks. I'm a Linux systems engineer, and this is where I design, break, fix, and document infrastructure. Everything here is real and running — Proxmox across two sites, ZFS storage on TrueNAS SCALE, OPNsense edge routing over XGS-PON fiber, a WireGuard overlay network, redundant DNS, and automated verified backups.
